# ClassPilot Privacy Policy

Privacy policy for the ClassPilot app.

Last updated: August 26, 2026

## Privacy Policy

ClassPilot helps users organize classes, create groups and seating charts, manage class rules and timetables, use interactive blackboards, and create notes, plans, and rich-text documents.

Users can enter names manually or extract names and timetable information from images using the device camera or images explicitly selected by the user.

## Data Collection

The ClassPilot developer does not collect or receive personal data, user-created content, or usage analytics, and ClassPilot does not sell such information. ClassPilot does not use third-party advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or data brokers.

Class information, names, groups, rules, seating charts, timetables, saved group sets, blackboards, notes, planning documents, imported media, and other user-created content are stored locally on the user’s device. The developer does not have access to this content.

The optional YouTube module is an exception to ClassPilot’s otherwise local operation. When a user requests a YouTube thumbnail or activates an embedded YouTube video, the user’s device connects directly to Google and YouTube. ClassPilot does not receive the resulting YouTube data. See **YouTube Module and Third-Party Processing** below.

Depending on the user’s device and Apple account settings, locally stored app data may be included in device backups or iCloud backups managed by Apple. The developer does not have access to these backups.

## Names, Groups, Rules, Timetables, and Seating Charts

ClassPilot processes information entered by the user, including names, group rules, saved groups, timetables, seating charts, seating arrangements, and related class information.

This information is used only to provide the app’s features and remains stored locally on the user’s device unless the user chooses to export or share it.

ClassPilot does not send class names, student names, rules, notes, seating information, or timetable contents to YouTube.

## Notes, Planning Documents, and Blackboards

Notes, planning templates, planning documents, and interactive blackboards are created and stored locally on the user’s device.

These items may contain formatted text, tables, drawings, images, local videos, YouTube links, and other content added by the user. The developer does not access, collect, or transmit their contents, except that the device must contact YouTube when the user requests a YouTube thumbnail or activates a YouTube video as described below.

Users are responsible for the information and content they choose to enter, import, display, or share through the app.

## YouTube Module and Third-Party Processing

ClassPilot includes an optional YouTube module that allows a user to enter a public YouTube link and display the referenced video through YouTube’s embedded player.

ClassPilot validates supported YouTube links, stores the link or video identifier locally as part of the blackboard, and does not send the link to the ClassPilot developer.

The YouTube player is not loaded automatically merely because a link has been saved. It is loaded when the user activates the video. If the user separately enables the thumbnail option, the device may contact YouTube’s image servers to retrieve the thumbnail before the player is activated.

When a thumbnail or embedded player is loaded, Google and YouTube may receive and process technical and usage information needed to provide, secure, personalize, advertise through, and measure the service. This may include:

- The user’s IP address
- Device and operating-system information
- App or browser information
- Request time
- The referring app identifier
- Cookies or similar session identifiers
- Player interactions
- Playback information

The precise information collected and its retention are controlled by Google and YouTube, not by ClassPilot or its developer.

ClassPilot uses YouTube’s privacy-enhanced embed host (`youtube-nocookie.com`) and a non-persistent WebKit data store. These measures limit persistent YouTube website data within ClassPilot between app sessions, but they do not prevent Google or YouTube from receiving or retaining information on their systems when their services are contacted.

Playback position retained by ClassPilot is session-only and is not saved as viewing history in the blackboard.

ClassPilot does not:

- Request access to a user’s Google or YouTube account
- Use Google Sign-In
- Collect Google or YouTube credentials
- Retrieve subscriptions, account history, or private account information
- Download YouTube videos
- Separate or extract audio from YouTube videos
- Suppress YouTube advertisements
- Obscure the standard YouTube player controls
- Intentionally permit hidden or background YouTube playback

YouTube playback is paused when the relevant slide or player is no longer visible or when the app leaves active use.

The embedded web view is restricted to the validated player page for the video chosen by the user and is not available as a general-purpose web browser. Normal YouTube player controls remain available.

A link explicitly selected by the user may be handed to the system browser or YouTube app. Any activity there takes place outside ClassPilot and is governed by the receiving service’s terms and privacy practices.

Use of the YouTube module is subject to the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Google account connections and permissions can be reviewed through [Google’s third-party connections page](https://myaccount.google.com/connections). ClassPilot does not request a Google account connection, so a ClassPilot authorization would not normally appear there.

ClassPilot is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by YouTube or Google.

YouTube videos are hosted, supplied, and controlled by YouTube and their respective uploaders. ClassPilot and its developer do not select, own, endorse, moderate, or guarantee the availability, accuracy, legality, age suitability, accessibility, advertisements, recommendations, or content of a video added by a user.

Users are responsible for ensuring that videos they add or display are appropriate for their audience and are used in accordance with applicable copyright rules, school policies, age requirements, local laws, and YouTube’s terms.

To the extent permitted by applicable law, the developer is not responsible for third-party content or for changes, interruptions, removals, restrictions, or processing performed independently by YouTube, Google, an uploader, or an external app. Nothing in this policy excludes responsibilities that cannot legally be excluded.

Users can stop further use of YouTube within ClassPilot by pausing the player, deleting the YouTube module or blackboard, and choosing not to activate YouTube content.

Deleting a module removes its locally stored link from that blackboard. It does not delete information that Google or YouTube may have processed under their own retention policies.

## Camera and Selected Images

If the user chooses to scan a class list or timetable, ClassPilot requests access to the device camera. Camera content is processed on the device to identify and extract relevant text.

The app can also process images explicitly selected by the user. Images used only for scanning are processed on the device and are not sent to the developer or third parties.

Images that the user intentionally inserts into a planning document or blackboard are stored locally as part of that content.

ClassPilot does not access images that the user has not selected or captured for use within the app.

## Microphone and Noise Meter

If the user activates a blackboard noise meter, ClassPilot requests microphone permission. Microphone input is processed on the device only while an active noise meter is running to estimate sound levels.

ClassPilot does not record, save, transmit, or provide microphone audio to the developer or third parties.

Temporary sound-level measurements and graph history are held only as needed for the active noise-meter session.

The user can stop microphone use by:

- Turning off the noise meter
- Closing the blackboard
- Revoking microphone permission in the device’s system settings
- Closing the app

The noise meter provides an estimate and is not a certified sound-level instrument.

## Import and Export

ClassPilot allows users to import and export classes, groups, rules, timetables, seating charts, blackboards, planning documents, PDFs, images, and other user-created content.

Exported files may contain names, links, or other information entered by the user. Users choose when, where, and with whom exported content is shared.

When a user shares or stores an exported file through another app or service, that app or service may process the file according to its own privacy policy.

ClassPilot and its developer do not control how third-party recipients or services handle content that the user chooses to share.

## Third-Party Services

Apart from the optional YouTube module described above, ClassPilot does not use third-party advertising SDKs, analytics services, or external data-collection services.

The app may use standard Apple system features for actions such as:

- Selecting images and files
- Accessing the camera or microphone with permission
- Opening and importing files
- Exporting content
- Displaying the system share sheet

These features are controlled by the user and the operating system.

Third-party services contacted at the user’s request process information under their own privacy policies and terms.

ClassPilot limits the information it sends to what is needed to provide the requested feature and does not provide locally stored class or student information to YouTube.

Where a third party processes information through a ClassPilot feature, it is expected to protect that information in accordance with its published privacy commitments and the protections required by applicable law.

## Data Retention and Deletion

User-created content remains stored locally until the user deletes it from within the app, removes the relevant files, or deletes the app and its locally stored data.

Copies exported or shared outside ClassPilot must be deleted separately from the location or service where the user stored them.

Information processed independently by YouTube, Google, Apple, or another external service is subject to that service’s retention and deletion practices.

ClassPilot does not maintain a developer account or server containing user content, so there is no server-side ClassPilot account data for the developer to retrieve or delete.

## Children and Student Information

The ClassPilot developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children or students.

Teachers and other users are responsible for ensuring that information entered, stored, exported, displayed, or shared through the app is handled in accordance with applicable school policies, privacy requirements, safeguarding duties, and local laws.

Users who display YouTube content to children or students are responsible for selecting age-appropriate content and complying with YouTube’s terms, applicable age restrictions, and relevant school or parental requirements.

ClassPilot does not create child accounts or provide student information to YouTube.
